Gravity Flip

2D puzzle-platformer built in Unity

Play Demo

Overview

Gravity Flip is a short 2D puzzle-platformer built in Unity where you invert gravity to solve puzzles. Every room is designed around the tension of vertical traversal: hazards become tools when the world turns upside-down, and timing matters as you flip between floor and ceiling.

Core Mechanics

  • Gravity Inversion: Flip between up and down on demand with cooldown-based timing.
  • Momentum Retention: Preserve horizontal speed through flips for long aerial paths.
  • Interactables: Buttons, doors, and moving platforms create layered puzzle states.
  • Combat: Find challenging enemies scattered across levels to fight hand to hand.

Levels and Puzzles

Tech

  • Unity + C# character controller with custom gravity vectors
  • Tilemap-based levels, triggers, and simple state machine for doors
  • Custom camera controller, post-processing, and lightweight UI
  • Advanced AI locomotion on a 2 dimensional plane
