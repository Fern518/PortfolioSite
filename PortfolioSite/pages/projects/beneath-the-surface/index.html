Beneath the Surface

A multiplayer dungeon-crawler horror game.

Project Overview

Beneath the Surface is a thrilling multiplayer dungeon-crawler horror game that immerses players in a dark and eerie environment. Players must work together to navigate through treacherous dungeons filled with terrifying creatures and challenging puzzles.

Features

  • Cooperative gameplay for up to four players.
  • Dynamic environments that change with each playthrough.
  • Variety of character classes with unique abilities.
  • Engaging storyline with multiple endings.

Technologies Used

The game was developed using:

  • Unity for game development.
  • C# for scripting and gameplay mechanics.
  • Blender for 3D modeling and animations.
  • FMOD for audio integration and sound design.

Gameplay Mechanics

Players must explore the dark dungeons, solve puzzles, and defeat enemies to progress. The game features a unique fear mechanic that affects player abilities and interactions based on their character's fear level.

Conclusion

Beneath the Surface offers a unique blend of horror and cooperative gameplay, making it a thrilling experience for players who enjoy challenges and teamwork.
