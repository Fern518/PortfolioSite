Tech Support Business

A project dedicated to providing tech support services on Orcas Island.

Project Overview

This project involved starting a tech support business aimed at helping residents of Orcas Island with their technology needs. The goal was to provide accessible and reliable tech support to individuals and small businesses.

Technologies Used

  • HTML/CSS for website development
  • JavaScript for interactive features
  • Various remote support tools
  • Social media platforms for marketing

Impact

The tech support business has successfully assisted numerous clients, improving their technology experience and resolving various tech-related issues. Feedback from clients has been overwhelmingly positive, highlighting the importance of accessible tech support in rural areas.

Future Plans

Plans for the future include expanding services to include workshops and training sessions for technology use, as well as developing an online presence to reach a broader audience.
